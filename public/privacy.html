Privacy Policy

Effective September 28, 2026

MD Viewer is developed and published by the MD Viewer project maintainer, ho-ben. This policy covers the MD Viewer Android app and web app.

Data handling

MD Viewer does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information, document contents, usage data, analytics, advertising identifiers, or crash reports. There are no accounts, advertisements, or tracking SDKs.

Files are accessed only after you explicitly open, paste, drop, or share them to MD Viewer. Rendering happens on your device. Open documents and reading positions may be saved in the app's private local storage so your tabs can be restored. This saved information is not available to the developer or other apps.

Permissions and network access

The native Android app requests no Android permissions, including no storage, media, or Internet permission. Android grants it temporary access only to a file you explicitly open or share. Libraries used to render Markdown, equations, code, and diagrams are bundled with the app.

The web app is hosted by GitHub Pages. Loading the site may expose ordinary web request information, such as an IP address and browser details, to GitHub under GitHub's own privacy terms. MD Viewer itself adds no analytics or tracking.

Retention and deletion

Closing a document tab removes that document from MD Viewer's saved session. You can remove all locally stored app data by clearing MD Viewer's storage or uninstalling the Android app. In a browser, clear the site's data through your browser settings. Because no document or account data is sent to the developer, there is no remote account or server-side document data to delete.

External links and children

Links inside documents can open other apps or websites, whose own privacy policies apply. MD Viewer is a general-purpose document utility and does not knowingly collect data from children or anyone else.

Changes and contact

Material changes will be posted on this page with a new effective date. For privacy questions, contact the maintainer through the MD Viewer issue tracker.
